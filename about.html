About FoodShare

FoodShare is a student prototype for food waste management. It helps donors list surplus food and allows NGOs to discover and claim available donations.

Objectives

  • Reduce avoidable food waste.
  • Connect donors with community organizations.
  • Make donation information easier to share.
  • Provide a foundation for AI-assisted matching and chatbot support.

Limitations: This demo does not include secure login, NGO verification, a trained ML model or a live Botpress integration.
